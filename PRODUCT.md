# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Friends and general visitors viewing a personal collection of sky photographs, music, and memories.

## Product Purpose

Present Derkey's photo diary as a calm, personal place that is easy to browse and pleasant to revisit.

## Brand Personality

Dreamy, cute, and simple. The experience should feel personal and gentle rather than polished like a company website.

## Anti-references

Avoid busy layouts, corporate landing-page patterns, luxury styling, excessive decoration, and dark or aggressive visual treatments.

## Design Principles

1. Let each photograph remain the main visual focus.
2. Use small delightful details only when they communicate meaning.
3. Keep controls obvious, comfortable, and visually quiet.
4. Preserve a soft, personal mood across motion, typography, and color.
5. Prefer a few consistent visual rules over many one-off effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, comfortable touch targets, descriptive image alternatives, and a reduced-motion experience.
